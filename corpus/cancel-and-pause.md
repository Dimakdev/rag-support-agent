# Cancelling and pausing

## Cancelling

Billing → Cancel subscription. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period already paid for;
nothing is charged after that. Until then everything keeps working.

The account is not deleted by cancelling. It becomes read-only at the end of the term and stays that
way for 60 days, which is enough time to export. After 60 days the data is scheduled for deletion and
cannot be recovered.

Only the Owner can cancel. An Admin sees the option but is asked to contact the Owner.

## Pausing instead

Seasonal businesses can pause instead of cancelling. A pause costs 9 CAD per month for the whole
account regardless of seat count, keeps all data, and blocks sign-in for everyone except the Owner.

A pause runs for whole months, minimum one and maximum three, and is allowed once per calendar year.
Pausing is available on Team and Business, not on Starter.

## Coming back

Resuming from a pause or from read-only puts the account back on the plan it had, at the price it had.
Reactivating after the 60-day window means starting over: a new account, no old data.

## Downgrading

A plan can be downgraded at renewal. If the account has more seats than the new plan allows, the
downgrade is refused until seats are removed. Downgrading from Business also switches SSO off, so
everyone signs in with a password again — which needs a password reset for anyone who never had one.
