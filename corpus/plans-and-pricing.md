# Plans and pricing

Harbourline is billed per seat, per month. A seat is one person who signs in — a technician who only
receives jobs on the mobile app still needs a seat.

## The three plans

### Starter

19 CAD per seat per month. Up to 10 seats. Job scheduling, the mobile app, email notifications, and
CSV export. No API access and no SSO.

### Team

39 CAD per seat per month. Up to 50 seats. Everything in Starter plus dispatch rules, recurring jobs,
SMS notifications, the QuickBooks and Xero integrations, and the API.

### Business

79 CAD per seat per month. No seat ceiling. Everything in Team plus SAML single sign-on, audit logs,
custom roles, a sandbox environment, and a named support contact.

## Annual billing

Paying for a year up front costs ten months instead of twelve. The discount applies to every seat on
the account and is shown on the invoice as "annual credit", not as a lower per-seat price.

Switching from monthly to annual takes effect immediately: the unused part of the current month is
credited against the annual invoice. Switching from annual back to monthly happens at renewal, not
mid-term.

## Seat minimums

Starter and Team have a minimum of 3 seats. Business has a minimum of 10 seats. Accounts below the
minimum are billed for the minimum, which is why removing seats sometimes does not change the bill.

## What is not charged

Customers of yours who receive a booking link, sign a job sheet, or open a job status page do not
need a seat and are never counted. Jobs archived more than 24 months ago do not count toward plan
limits.

## Taxes

Prices are before tax. GST or HST is added for Canadian accounts based on the billing address. Accounts
with a valid business number can enter it in Billing to have it appear on the invoice.
