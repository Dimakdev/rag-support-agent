# Privacy and where data lives

## Where it is stored

Accounts choose a region when they sign up: Canada or the European Union. Job data, customer records,
photos and backups stay in that region. The region cannot be changed after signup; moving an account
means an export and a fresh account.

## Who can see it

Support staff can read an account's data only after an explicit in-product grant from an Admin, which
lasts 7 days and is recorded in the audit log on Business plans. Without that grant, support sees
account metadata — plan, seats, invoice history — and nothing else.

## Subprocessors

Hosting, email delivery, SMS delivery, error reporting and payments each involve one named provider.
The current list is on the public subprocessors page and changes are announced 30 days ahead.

## Technician location

Location is recorded at two moments only: opening a job and completing it. There is no continuous
tracking. Location can be switched off per account, in which case job sheets record no coordinates at
all. This is deliberate: a product that quietly tracks people gets used to manage them, and that is
not what it is for.

## Customer data

Customers are not marketed to. Their contact details are used for job reminders and nothing else,
and a customer can ask for their record to be removed through any account that holds it.

## Retention

See Export and deletion for the exact windows. In short: live data for as long as the account exists,
60 days after it stops paying, 30 days after a deletion request, 35 days in backups after that.
