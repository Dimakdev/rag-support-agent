# Export and deletion

## Exporting

Settings → Data → Export. Jobs, customers, people and invoices, as CSV or JSON. An export of an
account with tens of thousands of jobs is prepared in the background and emailed as a link that is
valid for 7 days.

Photos and signatures are not in the CSV export. They come in a separate archive, requested the same
way, because a year of job photos is measured in gigabytes.

Export is available on every plan, including while an account is read-only. An account that cannot pay
can still get its data out — locking data behind a bill is not something we do.

## Deleting one customer's data

A customer record can be deleted from the customer page. Jobs for that customer keep their history but
lose the name, address and contact details, which are replaced with "removed at the customer's
request" and the date.

## Deleting the account

Settings → Data → Delete account. It asks for the Owner's password and a typed confirmation, then
schedules deletion for 30 days later. Cancelling the deletion inside those 30 days restores
everything; after that nothing is recoverable, including by support.

Backups are kept for a further 35 days for disaster recovery and are then overwritten. Data inside a
backup is not reachable through the product and is not restored selectively.

## Deletion requests under privacy law

A request from an individual — a technician, a customer — is answered within 30 days. Write to the
address in the privacy page with enough detail to find the record.
