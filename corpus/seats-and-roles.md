# Seats and roles

## The four roles

**Owner.** One per account. Can cancel, change the plan, move billing, and transfer ownership. Cannot
be removed while they are the Owner.

**Admin.** Everything except billing and ownership. Can add and remove seats, which does change the
bill, so admins are worth choosing carefully.

**Dispatcher.** Sees the whole schedule, creates and assigns jobs, cannot change settings or seats.

**Technician.** Sees only jobs assigned to them, on the mobile app and on the web. Cannot see other
people's schedules, customer lists, or prices unless the job sheet includes them.

Business accounts can build custom roles from the same permissions.

## Adding someone

Settings → People → Invite. The invitation is valid for 14 days. A seat is charged from the moment the
invitation is accepted, not when it is sent, and the first month is pro rata.

## Removing someone

Removing a person frees the seat at the end of the current period; the bill drops at the next invoice.
Their jobs stay where they are and keep their name in the history — removing a person never rewrites
past jobs.

Below the plan minimum the bill does not drop further. Three seats on Starter is the floor.

## Transferring ownership

Settings → People → the person → Make Owner. The old Owner becomes an Admin. Ownership cannot be
transferred to someone who has not signed in at least once.

## Someone left and nobody can get in

If the Owner is gone and unreachable, support can transfer ownership to another Admin after checking
the request against the billing contact on file. This is the one thing that always needs a human, and
it usually takes one business day.
