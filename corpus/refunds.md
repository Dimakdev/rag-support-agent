# Refunds

## Annual plans

An annual plan can be refunded in full within 30 days of the payment. Ask in Billing → Request refund,
or write to support. The money goes back to the card or account that paid, and banks take 5 to 10
business days to show it.

After 30 days an annual plan is not refunded. It can be cancelled, and cancelling stops the next
renewal while leaving access until the paid term ends.

## Monthly plans

A monthly plan is not refunded for the current month. Cancelling stops the next charge and access
continues to the end of the period already paid for.

## Seats added mid-cycle

Seats added in the middle of a month are charged pro rata. If a seat is removed within 14 days of
being added and was never signed in to, that charge is reversed on the next invoice as a credit
rather than a refund.

## Duplicate and failed charges

A charge that appears twice for the same period is always refunded, at any point in the term. A
charge for a period after an account was cancelled is refunded the same way. Send the invoice number
and support does the rest.

## Credits

A credit sits on the account and is used by the next invoice automatically. A credit can be converted
into a refund to the original payment method on request, as long as the payment it came from is less
than 12 months old.

## What is not refundable

SMS credits that have already been spent, and any month in which the account had an active job. Unused
SMS credits expire 12 months after purchase and are not refundable at expiry.
