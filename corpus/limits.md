# Limits

Numbers that decide whether something is possible, in one place.

## Per plan

| | Starter | Team | Business |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seats | 3 to 10 | 3 to 50 | 10 and up |
| Jobs per month | 500 | 5,000 | unlimited |
| Recurring series | — | 200 | unlimited |
| API keys | — | 5 | 25 |
| Territories | — | — | 50 |
| History kept | 24 months | 36 months | 36 months |

Going over the monthly job limit does not stop work: jobs keep being created and the billing contact
is told. Two consecutive months over the limit move the account to the next plan at the next renewal,
with notice.

## Per item

Attachment: 25 MB. Photos per job: 20. Checklist items per job: 100. Notes per job: unlimited, 5,000
characters each. Customers per account: unlimited.

## Rate limits

API: 120 requests per minute per key. Booking links: 60 submissions per hour per link, which stops a
script from filling a schedule with rubbish. Export: one full export per hour.

## Retention

Cancelled jobs are visible for 30 days, then archived and still searchable. Job history is kept for
the period in the table above, counted from the job's date. Original-resolution photos: 30 days.
Audit log: 12 months. Backups: 35 days.
