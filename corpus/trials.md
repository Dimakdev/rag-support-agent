# Trials

## What the trial includes

14 days on the Team plan, with up to 5 seats. No card is asked for at signup. Everything on Team works
during the trial except SMS notifications, which need bought credits, and the API, which is enabled on
request so that a trial cannot be used to run a production integration for free.

## When it ends

On day 15 the account becomes read-only until a plan is chosen. Nothing is deleted. Data stays for
60 days, the same window as a cancelled account.

A trial can be extended once by 7 days. Ask support before it expires; an expired trial is not
extended, but choosing a plan restores everything anyway.

## Turning a trial into a paid account

Billing → Choose plan. The account keeps every job, customer and setting created during the trial.
Billing starts the day the plan is chosen, not the day the trial started.

## One trial per organisation

Trials are counted per organisation, not per email address. A second trial for the same business is
refused; support can grant an exception for a genuinely separate entity, such as a franchise operating
under its own business number.
